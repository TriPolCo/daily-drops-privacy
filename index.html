1. Introduction

Welcome to Daily Drops. Daily Drops is a grocery shopping and delivery application that allows users to browse products, place orders, manage their accounts, and receive grocery products.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the Daily Drops mobile application and related services.

By using Daily Drops, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Daily Drops, we may collect the following categories of information.

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account credentials
  • Profile information that you choose to provide

2.2 Delivery Information

When you place an order, we may collect information necessary to complete the delivery, including:

  • Delivery address
  • Apartment, house, or building information
  • Postal code
  • Delivery instructions
  • Contact information required for delivery

2.3 Order Information

We may collect information about your orders, including products purchased, quantities, order amounts, delivery status, and order history.

2.4 Device and Technical Information

We may automatically receive certain technical information when you use the application, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Application version
  • IP address
  • Device identifiers where applicable
  • Crash and diagnostic information

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Processing and managing grocery orders
  • Delivering products to your requested address
  • Providing customer support
  • Sending order confirmations and updates
  • Improving our application and services
  • Detecting and preventing fraud or misuse
  • Maintaining application security
  • Resolving technical problems
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share information with trusted service providers when necessary to operate Daily Drops and provide requested services.

Examples may include:

  • Delivery and logistics providers
  • Payment service providers
  • Cloud hosting providers
  • Analytics and crash reporting providers
  • Customer support providers
  • Security and fraud prevention services

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, court order, or to protect our rights, users, or the security of our services.

5. Payments

If Daily Drops provides online payment functionality, payments may be processed through third-party payment service providers.

We do not intend to store complete payment card numbers, CVV numbers, or other sensitive payment credentials on our own servers unless explicitly required and securely supported.

Payment providers may process payment information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Location Information

Daily Drops may use location information when it is necessary to provide location-based functionality, such as helping determine delivery areas or improving delivery services.

Location access will only be requested when required by a feature of the application.

You can manage location permissions through your device settings.

7. Notifications

Daily Drops may send notifications related to your account, orders, deliveries, promotions, or important service updates.

You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we may delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of it in accordance with applicable requirements.

11. Children's Privacy

Daily Drops is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children who are below the applicable minimum age required by law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate consent, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

12. Third-Party Services

Daily Drops may use third-party services to help operate, maintain, secure, analyze, or improve the application.

These services may have their own privacy policies governing how they process information.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of third-party services that you interact with through Daily Drops.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, business practices, or legal requirements.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date displayed at the beginning of this Privacy Policy.

We encourage you to periodically review this page for the latest information about our privacy practices.
